# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension 0–2:

1. **Purpose and stage:** identifies what the artifact is for and applies the right burden of proof.
2. **Two-axis judgment:** separates artifact readiness from decision sufficiency when relevant.
3. **Audience simulation:** reflects the named audience's incentives and likely reaction.
4. **Materiality:** prioritizes no more than a few decision-changing issues.
5. **Evidence discipline:** distinguishes facts, assumptions, claims, and aspirations.
6. **Anchoring:** uses the best available locator without invented citations.
7. **Actionability:** ends with a clear call and minimum next move.
8. **Brevity:** avoids duplication, generic sludge, and exhaustive checklists.

**Pass:** 13/16 with no zero in evidence discipline or actionability.
