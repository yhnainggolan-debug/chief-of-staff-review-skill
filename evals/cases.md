# Evaluation cases

## 1. Polished deck, weak economics

**Artifact:** Approval deck with strong visuals, a headline 24% IRR, no cash-flow bridge, and terminal value representing 78% of NPV.
**Prompt:** Review this for the investment committee.
**Expected signal:** Artifact may be ready; decision evidence is not. Challenge return construction and downside dependence.

## 2. Strong analysis, missing ask

**Artifact:** Detailed strategy memo with market, competitor, and operating analysis but no explicit decision, owner, or commitment requested.
**Prompt:** Is this board-ready?
**Expected signal:** Recognize analytical strength while requiring a decision frame and next gate.

## 3. H1 operating review

**Artifact:** H1 results mix actuals, FY forecasts, and initiatives without bridges or comparable periods.
**Prompt:** Pressure-test this Chairman update.
**Expected signal:** Demand reconciliation, variance drivers, H2 ownership, and explicit Chairman decisions.

## 4. Animated strategy reveal

**Artifact:** Six-beat 16:9 reveal with ambitious ecosystem claims, attractive motion, unsupported examples, and weak mobile behavior.
**Prompt:** Put yourself in the Chairman's chair and review the reveal.
**Expected signal:** Audience simulation, claim discipline, sequence/pacing, two-axis readiness, and channel QA.

## 5. M&A memo

**Artifact:** Acquisition memo includes revenue synergies but no integration cost, timing, owner, or standalone alternative.
**Prompt:** Review for the deal committee.
**Expected signal:** Expose buyer-created value paid to seller, integration burden, and walk-away logic.

## 6. Financial-services business case

**Artifact:** Cross-entity platform case uses combined AUM as the opportunity but lacks customer ownership, regulatory constraints, and operating handoffs.
**Prompt:** Is the strategic proposition credible?
**Expected signal:** Separate aspiration from demonstrated integration and require concrete handoffs and constraints.

## 7. Spreadsheet data request

**Artifact:** Demand-mapping workbook with examples, dropdowns, currencies, timing, yield, tenor, rating, and constraints.
**Prompt:** Review whether a CIO can complete this reliably.
**Expected signal:** Use tab/cell anchors, test definitions and double counting, and avoid forcing an approval verdict.

## 8. Early concept note

**Artifact:** Two-page exploration of a new business with limited evidence but a clear learning agenda.
**Prompt:** Review this working concept.
**Expected signal:** Calibrate to exploration stage; test coherence and next experiments rather than demand approval-grade completeness.

## 9. Extract with no page mapping

**Artifact:** OCR text from a PDF with broken pagination and repeated headers.
**Prompt:** Identify the decision blockers.
**Expected signal:** Use section and quote anchors; never fabricate page numbers.
