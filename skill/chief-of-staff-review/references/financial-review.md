# Financial and investment review

Use when economics, valuation, returns, capital, funding, liquidity, forecasts, or transaction terms are material.

## Reconcile the model

Check:

- sources and uses balance
- operating forecasts reconcile with cash flow
- valuation inputs reconcile with the stated method
- debt, interest, fees, taxes, working capital, capex, and transaction costs are included
- nominal versus real, pre-tax versus post-tax, and levered versus unlevered measures are labeled
- currency, units, timing conventions, and FX assumptions are consistent
- historical actuals, management forecasts, and scenarios are not blended

Recompute key arithmetic where possible. Treat unexplained non-reconciliation as a decision issue, not a formatting issue.

## Test the baseline

Every value claim needs a comparison case. Identify the baseline, its evidence, and whether it is realistic. Ask “compared with what?” for growth, savings, returns, market share, and strategic benefit.

## Returns and valuation

Inspect:

- revenue and margin bridge
- cash conversion and working-capital needs
- capex and maintenance requirements
- terminal value or exit multiple dependence
- discount rate and risk premium
- dilution, ownership, and value attribution
- payback, NPV, IRR, MOIC, or other relevant measures
- sensitivity to the few assumptions that drive most value

Do not accept a headline return without the cash-flow path behind it.

## Funding, liquidity, and downside

Test:

- committed versus indicative funding
- tenor, amortization, covenants, security, and refinancing exposure
- liquidity runway and peak funding need
- currency and rate mismatch
- base, downside, and severe-but-plausible cases
- breach points, stop-loss conditions, and reversibility
- capital or regulatory constraints

## Evidence discipline

Label estimates and provisional assumptions. Do not imply approval, market availability, or pricing where only a working target exists. If the model cannot be inspected, say what cannot be validated and narrow the verdict accordingly.
