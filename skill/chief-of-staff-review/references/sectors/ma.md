# M&A and corporate finance lens

Test:

- strategic rationale versus standalone alternatives
- quality of earnings and normalized cash flow
- valuation bridge and control premium
- synergy ownership, timing, cost, tax, and double counting
- financing certainty, covenants, dilution, and downside
- diligence gaps and conditions precedent
- integration thesis, governance, talent retention, and Day 1 readiness
- exit, walk-away price, and negotiation leverage

Separate value the buyer creates from value already paid to the seller.
