# Renewables and power lens

Test:

- resource assumptions and independent yield assessment
- degradation, availability, curtailment, and grid connection
- PPA tariff, tenor, indexation, take-or-pay, and counterparty quality
- EPC scope, guarantees, liquidated damages, and completion risk
- land, permits, environmental approvals, and interconnection rights
- capex, contingencies, replacement capex, and O&M
- debt sizing, DSCR, refinancing, and currency mismatch
- merchant tail and downside generation cases

Separate technically feasible generation from contracted, financeable cash flow.
