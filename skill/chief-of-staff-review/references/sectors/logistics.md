# Logistics and infrastructure lens

Test:

- contracted versus forecast volume
- asset utilization, turnaround time, bottlenecks, and network effects
- capex, maintenance, residual value, and replacement cycle
- customer concentration and take-or-pay strength
- regulatory, concession, route, and cross-border constraints
- fuel, labor, FX, and financing sensitivity
- commissioning, interoperability, and operational readiness
- service-level obligations and downside underutilization

Distinguish capacity from usable, contracted, and economically attractive throughput.
