# Financial services lens

Test the proposition across customer, product, balance-sheet, risk, and regulatory realities:

- customer ownership, distribution economics, retention, and cross-sell evidence
- investable asset or product shelf versus actual demand
- AUM, flows, yield, spread, persistency, and benchmark definitions
- ALM, duration, liquidity, concentration, rating, seniority, and currency mismatch
- capital, solvency/RBC, eligibility, and approval constraints
- conflicts between origination, manufacturing, distribution, and fiduciary duties
- data, suitability, governance, and accountability across entities

For ecosystem claims, require a concrete handoff, owner, value pool, and proof point. Shared branding or arrows between entities are not operating integration.
