# Mining lens

Test:

- resource/reserve basis, confidence category, and mine life
- production ramp, recovery, strip ratio, dilution, and geological risk
- commodity price, FX, royalties, logistics, and cost-curve assumptions
- sustaining versus growth capex and closure obligations
- permits, land, community, environmental, and water constraints
- infrastructure dependencies and evacuation capacity
- contractor, equipment, power, and working-capital readiness
- downside economics at lower price, grade, recovery, and volume

Do not let headline resources substitute for an executable, permitted, funded mine plan.
