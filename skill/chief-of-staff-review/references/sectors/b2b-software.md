# B2B software lens

Test:

- painful customer problem and budget owner
- ICP, sales cycle, pipeline quality, win rate, and implementation burden
- ARR/MRR definitions, retention, expansion, churn, and concentration
- gross margin after cloud, support, and service delivery
- CAC, payback, channel economics, and sales productivity
- product differentiation, switching costs, security, and compliance
- roadmap dependencies, technical debt, and support scalability
- cash runway and credible milestones to the next funding or profitability gate

Do not treat pilots, signed LOIs, bookings, and recurring revenue as interchangeable.
